OUR STORY

Family recipes, cooked the slow way

Spice Route Kitchen started as a weekend supper club before opening its doors on FC Road. Every recipe on this menu has been in the family for at least two generations.

We source vegetables daily from the Pune wholesale market and grind our own spice blends every morning.
