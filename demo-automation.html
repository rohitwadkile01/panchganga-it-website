HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, running quietly in the background

1. Reads your export

Connects to the Excel or CSV file you already generate — no new software for your team to learn.

2. Runs the numbers

Totals, top items, and day-over-day trends are calculated automatically, the same way every time.

3. Delivers the report

Sent to WhatsApp or email on a schedule — the summary is waiting for you, not the other way round.
